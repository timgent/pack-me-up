# Key Features

## Smart packing lists
Answer a few questions about your trip and get a tailored packing list — climate, duration, activities, who's travelling. No generic checklists.

## Your questions, your rules
Define exactly what gets asked before each trip. Add, edit, or remove questions and control which items they trigger.

## Local-first, always available
Everything is stored in your browser via PouchDB. Works offline, no account required to get started.

## Solid Pod sync
Connect a Solid Pod to back up and sync your data across devices. You own it — not us.

## Share lists
Send a packing list to someone else via their Solid Pod. Grant or revoke access whenever you like.

## Browse shared lists
View lists shared with you or explore packing lists on other people's pods.

## Backups
Export and restore your questions and lists. Take your data anywhere.
